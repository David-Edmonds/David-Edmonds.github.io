FEATURED ANALYSIS · EXCEL · FICTIONAL DATA

Sales & Profitability

Sales are growing. Is profit keeping pace?

I built an Excel dashboard to compare revenue, gross profit and performance against plan. The supporting analysis separates growth from margin effects and reconciles both to the source totals.

KEY FINDING · FICTIONAL 2025 RESULTS

Revenue grew 12.7%, but gross profit grew 7.9%. A 1.4 percentage-point margin decline explains the gap.

Federal Contracting Performance

Where are federal awards concentrated, and how does participation vary?

I modeled federal award activity in Power BI, with DAX measures and agency and fiscal-year comparisons. The case study explains why small-business participation and goal attainment require different denominators.

ANALYTICAL CONSIDERATION

Participation and goal attainment answer different questions. Their denominators must be understood before comparing the measures.

TABLEAU CASE STUDY

Washington EV Market Overview

How are registered EVs distributed across Washington?

I designed a Tableau dashboard to compare counties, manufacturers, vehicle types and model years. Filters support regional comparisons; the case study explains what registration counts can and cannot tell us.

ANALYTICAL CONSIDERATION

A county with more registered EVs does not necessarily have a higher adoption rate. Population context matters.
