Washington EV
Market Overview

An interactive executive dashboard designed to make Washington State electric-vehicle registration data understandable, explorable, and useful.

Explore dashboard ↓Open on Tableau Public ↗View project files ↗
THE QUESTION

How is electric-vehicle adoption changing across Washington’s counties, manufacturers, vehicle types, and model years?

THE APPROACH

Structure the analysis around a clear KPI hierarchy, responsive filters, geographic context, market share, and explicit data-quality controls.

THE RESULT

A decision-ready view that makes regional differences, adoption patterns, and market leaders visible without overwhelming the user.

KEY FINDINGS

What the evidence tells us.

Published snapshot labeled July 30, 2026 · all filters cleared. The live dashboard may change; these findings refer to the linked image.

114,494 Teslas

One make leads the snapshot

Tesla represents about 41.0% of the 279,081 vehicles shown in the published, unfiltered dashboard snapshot.

Decision to inform

Explore manufacturer concentration by county and vehicle type before applying a statewide pattern locally.

July 30, 2026 dashboard snapshot ↗
King County

Volume is geographically concentrated

King is the top county by registered EV count in the snapshot. The map displays counts, rather than population-adjusted adoption rates.

Decision to inform

Use counts for scale; add population or total-vehicle denominators before comparing adoption penetration.

County map and top-county KPI ↗
60,091 · MY2023

Model year describes fleet composition

Model year 2023 has the largest cohort in the snapshot, at 60,091 vehicles. These are model years of registered vehicles, not registration dates.

Decision to inform

Do not interpret the lower counts for newer model years as a measured decline in annual registrations.

DESIGN DECISIONS

Built for clarity,
not decoration.

Lead with the market

Top-level KPIs answer the first questions quickly before the user moves into detailed exploration.

Make filters purposeful

Controls align with meaningful business dimensions rather than exposing every available field.

Protect interpretation

Data-quality checks and clear labels reduce the risk of drawing conclusions from incomplete or inconsistent values.
