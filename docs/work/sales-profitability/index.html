THE QUESTION

Are higher sales translating into stronger profit—and where is performance falling short?

THE APPROACH

Compare matching periods, separate growth and margin effects, and reconcile every contribution to the underlying totals.

THE DELIVERABLE

A one-page Excel dashboard with four filters, three editable charts, supporting calculations and a fictional source table.

KEY FINDINGS

What the evidence tells us.

Fictional Northstar Supply data · full-year 2025 vs 2024 · all regions and channels. These findings describe the sample, not client outcomes.

12.7% vs 7.9%

Revenue outpaces profit

2025 revenue reached $8.24M, up 12.7%. Gross profit reached $2.57M, up 7.9%; margin fell about 1.4 percentage points to 31.2%.

Decision to inform

Review margin alongside sales growth before judging performance.
